✦ Our Story

How we got here

Founded

An idea born from a copied portfolio

Mintique started after our founding team watched a friend's digital art get resold under someone else's name — with no way to prove otherwise.

Building

The authentication engine

We spent the first year on one problem: making every minted asset provably unique, before adding a single marketplace feature.

Launch

Opening the marketplace

With authentication solid, we opened Mintique to creators and collectors — pairing verified minting with instant token swaps.

Today

Thousands of verified assets

Mintique now supports a growing community of creators and collectors trading with confidence, backed by a Token ID for every piece.
